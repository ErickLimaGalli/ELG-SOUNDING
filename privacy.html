Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026.

Overview

ELG SOUNDING is an atmospheric sounding application that uses ERA5 data provided through the Copernicus Climate Data Store (CDS).

Copernicus CDS credentials

The application may ask you to provide your own Copernicus CDS personal API token. This credential is used to authenticate requests to the Copernicus Climate Data Store. The application does not include a shared developer CDS token.

Local data

Generated soundings, cached ERA5 files and application settings may be stored locally on your device. You can remove these files by deleting them or uninstalling the application.

Third-party services

ELG SOUNDING communicates with Copernicus / ECMWF services to request ERA5 data. Map Search may also load map resources from third-party map providers. Their own privacy policies and terms may apply.
